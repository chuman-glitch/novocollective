Nōvo Collective is an independent curatorial studio for contemporary art in public space and live settings. Founded in Los Angeles, we create uncommissioned and Work in Progress, and work alongside museums, foundations and cities internationally.

(01) Pillar Ongoing — Edition 03 in development

uncommissioned

Artists reclaim public space with temporary interventions that live beyond permissions, institutions and the white wall. Every edition takes on a new landscape, shaped by the people and place that host it.
